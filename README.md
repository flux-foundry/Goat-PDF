# GoatPDF 🐐
*Fastest local visual lore to PDF converter. No cap.*

## Overview
A lightning-fast, clutter-free image-to-PDF converter. Features built-in compression to easily bypass strict 1MB/2MB portal upload limits. 100% local processing. 

## The Lore (Why I built this)
Picture this: you've just finished an exam, the clock is ticking, and the internet is jammed. You need to upload your answer sheets *now*, but typical online PDF converters are bloated, slow, and a nightmare to navigate on mobile. Even outside of high-stress exam situations, waiting for mainstream sites to load or act funky is just annoying. 

I built GoatPDF so I could drop a file and get a PDF back in 2 seconds flat. I also decided to lean fully into an unapologetic "Gen Z" aesthetic because why make a boring, ordinary utility when you can make it fun? It's fast, private, and has the exact minimal options needed to get the job done without the clutter.

## Features
* **Blink-and-You-Miss-It Speed:** Converts and downloads your PDFs in under 2 seconds.
* **Institution Limits:** One-click presets to force file sizes under 1MB or 2MB for strict college and government portals.
* **The Rename Flex:** Bulk rename your individual drops directly inside the app before cooking the PDF—no more weird default file names.
* **100% Local & Secure:** Zero servers involved. Your documents never leave your device.
* **Unapologetic UI:** A playful, creative interface that actually makes converting documents entertaining.
